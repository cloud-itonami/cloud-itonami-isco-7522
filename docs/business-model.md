# Business Model: Cabinet-Making Workshop Scheduling and Logistics Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7522`
- ISCO-08: `7522`
- Occupation: Cabinet-makers and Related Workers
- Social impact: worker-safety, respiratory-health, industrial-continuity

## Customer

- workshop operators / cabinet-making contractors
- independent cabinet-making crews / crew cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- job/commission/progress-record logging
- timber/hardware-materials supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to shop safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a woodworking-execution decision (e.g. deciding
  to proceed with a specific cutting operation), ever
- no direct finalization of a workshop-safety-clearance decision (e.g.
  declaring the workshop safety cleared for operation), ever
- no override of a shop safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (power-tool hazard, wood-dust exposure,
  equipment condition) always route to human sign-off, regardless of
  confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
